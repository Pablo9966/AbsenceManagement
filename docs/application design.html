Design Principles
01
Warm intelligence
Data-dense interfaces don't have to be cold. Terracotta and linen replace sterile greys. The interface feels like a well-designed physical workspace — warm materials, precise tools.
From Claude
02
Hardware-grade craft
Every radius, shadow, and spacing value is intentional. Spring physics on interactions. Consistent 8px grid. The obsession Apple applies to physical products, applied here to pixels.
From Apple
03
B2B without compromise
Professional-grade data density without sacrificing approachability. Charts communicate at a glance. AI surfaces exactly when useful. The tool earns trust through clarity, not complexity.
Fusion
